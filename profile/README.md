### Megafires are not surprises. They are predictable.

The conditions behind a megafire build quietly over many years: fuel accumulates, vegetation dries, moisture declines, and all of it is visible from orbit. ForestSATai combines **AI**, **satellite Earth Observation** and **forest science** in one platform, turning more than a decade of satellite data into decisions that institutions can act on before the spark.

### One lifecycle. Five integrated products.

| Stage | Product | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Predict** | Wildfire Insight™ | Maps where fuel and fire risk are building, years before ignition, from 10+ years of EO data and 50+ environmental variables |
| **Prevent** | Wildfire Guardian™ | Turns risk intelligence into ranked, year-round prevention plans: fuel management, firebreaks and priorities |
| **Verify** | MVR™ (Monitor · Verify · Report) | Satellite, aerial, drone and LiDAR evidence that preventive work was done, and whether risk actually fell |
| **Respond** | Wildfire Response™ | Predicts fire direction, speed, intensity and flame height so crews can act ahead of the flame front |
| **Recover** | Wildfire Recovery™ | Post-fire damage assessment and long-term regeneration planning |

### Technology foundation

- **Satellite EO:** Sentinel-2/3, Landsat, SAR, MODIS, GOES-18 and commercial constellations, from 375 m to 10 m resolution
- **AI / ML:** risk prediction, fire-spread simulation and fuel-load mapping, GPU-accelerated
- **Forest science:** fuel accumulation, canopy structure, vegetation stress and drought signatures
- **Cloud SaaS:** geospatial processing, dashboards, API-ready outputs and role-based access
